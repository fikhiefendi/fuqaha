Academic work on Islamic law is scattered across languages and catalogues. A doctoral thesis in Turkey, a critical edition from the Maghrib and an article in English often treat the same question without knowing of one another.

The bibliography gathers this work into a single list.

## What is recorded?

- Doctoral and master's theses
- Peer-reviewed articles and conference papers
- Books and encyclopaedia entries
- Critical editions and translations

Each entry records its language, year, place of publication and subjects. Where a work concerns a particular jurist, it is linked to that jurist's page.

## How to filter

The list can be narrowed by type, language, range of years, school and subject. Search covers both the original title and its translation, so an Arabic title can also be found with an English keyword.

## How to contribute

You can suggest a missing work through the "Contribute" page. Complete bibliographic details help it get added quickly.
