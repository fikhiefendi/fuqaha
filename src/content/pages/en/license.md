## Fuqaha’s own content

Content prepared by Fuqaha is published under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) licence: the Turkish and English summaries, transcriptions, date conversions, place and teacher–student matches, bibliography records and essays. You may copy, redistribute and adapt it, including for commercial purposes, as long as you give credit.

## What is not covered

| Content | Status |
| --- | --- |
| The Arabic text of al-Fawa’id | The work itself is out of copyright. The digital text on the site comes from the OpenITI corpus; please check OpenITI’s terms before reusing it. |
| Coordinates | al-Ṯurayyā Gazetteer, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). |
| Base map | Esri; its own terms apply. |
| Images | Each image has its own licence; see [Image credits](../credits/). |
