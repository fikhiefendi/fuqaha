## Purpose

Learning about a jurist usually means consulting several biographical dictionaries, and finding the scholarship on him means searching separate catalogues in several languages. Fuqaha tries to reduce that scatter. Every statement is tied to its source by volume, page and entry number, so readers can check it for themselves.

## Sections

- **Jurists.** The 525 entries of al-Laknawi’s *al-Fawa’id al-bahiyya fi tarajim al-Hanafiyya*, each with a short summary in Turkish and English, the Arabic source text, teachers, students, works and places.
- **Map.** Where the jurists were born, studied, taught, served as judges and died, filterable by century.
- **Teacher–student network.** The chains of learning built from the teachers and students named in the entries.
- **Bibliography.** Doctoral theses on Islamic law, to be extended with articles, books and critical editions.

## Method

**Transcription.** Biographies rely on the source text alone; nothing is added from elsewhere. Information the source does not give (a year of birth, for instance) is left blank.

**Names.** Jurists’ names are given in a simple transcription for readability; the Arabic original accompanies every entry.

**Dates.** Hijri dates from the source are given with their Common Era equivalents, as in “534/1139-40”. Equivalents are computed with the tabular Islamic calendar; where month and day are unknown, two CE years are shown.

**Page numbers.** Page references are to the Cairo edition of 1324.

**Teacher–student links.** A name in one entry is linked to another entry only when it contains that entry’s name word for word and in the same order, father’s name included, and the two dates of death fit. Uncertain names are left unlinked, so the network is incomplete but aims to contain no false links.

## Citing

Every jurist entry ends with a ready-made citation in ISNAD and Chicago style. The site as a whole may be cited as:

> Fuqaha: A research archive on the lives, cities and works of Muslim jurists. https://fikhiefendi.github.io/fuqaha/
